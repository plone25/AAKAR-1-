# AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant

This application is designed to help rural micro-entrepreneurs make informed business decisions by providing:
1. Hyper-local business feasibility reports based on geographic location
2. Smart financial calculator that determines loan eligibility and repayment schedules

## Features

### Module 1: Hyper-Local Business Feasibility Report
- Market Reach Analysis (5-10 km radius)
- Opportunity Analysis (identifying underserved niches)
- SWOT Analysis tailored to micro-enterprise budget
- Threats Identification (supply chain, seasonal fluctuations, etc.)
- Competitor Mapping (density of similar businesses)
- Product Market Value (optimal pricing strategies)

### Module 2: Smart Financial Calculator & Scheme Router
- Automatic calculation of total feasible Project Cost (Available Margin / 10%)
- Maximum Loan Amount calculation (90% of Project Cost)
- Scheme Auto-Selection based on Project Cost:
  - Micro Finance Scheme: ≤ ₹1.40 lakh (6.5% interest, 3-year tenure, 3-month moratorium)
  - Term Loan Scheme: > ₹1.40 lakh and ≤ ₹50.00 lakh (8% interest, 7-year tenure, 6-month moratorium)
- EMI & Moratorium Generator with quarterly repayment schedule
